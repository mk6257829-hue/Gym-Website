TRAIN SMART

SAFE WEIGHT GUIDE

Build strength gradually and focus on proper technique.

01

Start Light

Beginners should start with bodyweight or the lightest comfortable resistance.

02

Correct Form

Learn the correct movement before increasing the weight.

03

Slow Progress

Increase resistance gradually only when you can control every repetition.

04

Take Rest

Allow muscles to recover between challenging workout sessions.

IMPORTANT SAFETY WARNING

Never lift more weight than you can safely control. Incorrect technique and excessive resistance can cause injuries. Stop exercising if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain or unusual shortness of breath. Ask a qualified trainer for guidance.
